Cases Component

R. v. Morgan
Successfully challenged a police search involving racial profiling, resulting in the exclusion of a firearm discovered on the client.

R. v. McIntyre
Secured an acquittal on appeal after establishing that blood sample evidence was obtained in breach of the client's right to counsel.

R. v. Ellis
Successfully challenged an unlawful vehicle search, resulting in the exclusion of fentanyl and cocaine discovered in the client's trunk.

R. v. Khan
Successfully challenged a search warrant, established a breach of the client's right to counsel, and secured the exclusion of critical evidence, undermining the prosecution's case.

R. v. Johnson and Bancroft
Secured an acquittal on all charges in a home invasion robbery case by challenging the credibility and reliability of the Crown's principal witness.

R. v. DaSilva Smith
Secured an acquittal on charges of attempted murder, aggravated assault, and robbery by raising self-defence and challenging inconsistencies in the complainant's testimony.

R. v. Ahmed
Successfully challenged the constitutionality of the five-year mandatory minimum sentence for human trafficking, leading the court to strike it down as cruel and unusual punishment.

R. v. Madeley
Secured a favourable ruling in a constitutional challenge to the mandatory victim surcharge, highlighting the disproportionate financial burden it placed on vulnerable offenders.

Case Library

Racial Profiling and Unlawful Search
Successfully challenged a police traffic stop and subsequent search that led to the discovery of a firearm on the client. The court found that unconscious racial bias influenced the police encounter and that multiple Charter rights had been violated. As a result, the firearm was excluded from evidence.

Impaired Driving Conviction Overturned
Successfully appealed an impaired driving conviction by establishing that blood sample evidence had been improperly admitted following violations of the client's Charter rights. The court excluded the evidence, overturned the conviction and entered an acquittal.

Unlawful Vehicle Search and Drug Seizure
The client faced multiple drug trafficking-related charges after police discovered fentanyl, cocaine and other controlled substances during a vehicle search. A successful challenge to the legality of the search resulted in the exclusion of all evidence obtained.

Unlawful Searches and Denial of Counsel
The client faced 27 charges in connection with a series of alleged armed robberies in Toronto. Successfully challenged an unlawful police search and established breaches of the client's right to counsel. The court found serious Charter violations and bad faith on the part of the lead investigator, resulting in the exclusion of key physical and surveillance evidence.

Armed Home Invasion Acquittal
The client faced charges of armed robbery, conspiracy and other offences in connection with an alleged home invasion. The defence challenged the credibility and reliability of the Crown’s key witness. The court found that the Crown had failed to prove guilt beyond a reasonable doubt and acquitted both defendants on all counts.
